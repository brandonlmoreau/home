# Construction Tracker

Clock in / clock out time tracker for jobsite hours.

- Weeks run **Monday → Sunday**
- Current week totals update live while you are clocked in
- Past weeks stay under **Past weeks** (saved on the phone)
- Works offline after install / download

Open from the [Home](../) screen, or go directly to this folder.

## On your Android phone

1. Open Home (or this page) in **Chrome**
2. Tap the menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**
3. Or tap **Download app file** for `construction-tracker.html`

Hours are stored in this browser/app on your phone. Use **Download backup** for a JSON copy.
